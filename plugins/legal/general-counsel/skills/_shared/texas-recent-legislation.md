## Tool grounding

Ground every Texas enactment live before it leaves a consult; this index tells you where the specialist files looked, the tool result is what you cite.

- Bills and session law: `legal_state_legislation` with `jurisdiction: "TX"`, the bill designator, and the session (`SB 29`, 89R; `SB 16`, 892). Read the enrolled text and the TLO history line for the effective date; a caption or a signing date is not an effective date, and a bill conditioned on a constitutional amendment needs the LRL amendments row for the adoption result.
- Codified text and the session-law chapter: `legal_statute_lookup` with `jurisdiction: "TX"` and the section in Bluebook form (`"Tex. Bus. Orgs. Code § 21.552"`); the credit line ("Added by Acts 2025, 89th Leg., R.S., Ch. 21 (S.B. 29)") supplies the chapter number carried in the tables below. Record the site's currency label next to the section.
- Constitution: statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/CN/htm/CN.8.htm for operative text and the "(Added Nov. 4, 2025.)" annotation; lrl.texas.gov/legis/constamends for the adoption result; the Governor's canvass proclamation for the effective date: Proclamation 41-4245, 50 Tex. Reg. 7779 (Dec. 5, 2025), https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/December52025/The%20Governor/The%20Governor.html, certifying the Secretary of State's tabulation on November 19, 2025.
- Litigation affecting an enactment: `legal_caselaw_search` with the forum, party names and docket number from this file; read the last docket entry on CourtListener before stating that an injunction stands or a stay is in force.

## Sponsor angles

Each angle rests only on a VERIFIED bullet above. Mechanism, authority, the counterparty's best counter, what defeats it.

- **Derivative-suit ownership floor** (BOC 21.552(a)(3) bullet). A sponsor-controlled Texas corporation with exchange-listed common shares, or one electing sec. 21.419 with 500 or more shareholders, writes a threshold up to three percent of outstanding shares into its certificate or bylaws; holders below it cannot institute the proceeding. Counter: the plaintiff pleads the proceeding was instituted before May 14, 2025 (sec. 26), or attacks the 500-shareholder count behind the election. Defeated by a private company with no listing and no election, or a holder group that aggregates to the floor.
- **Business-court forum clause** (Gov't Code 25A.004 bullet). Purchase, shareholder and credit agreements select the business court; jurisdiction attaches to governance, fiduciary and BOC claims over $5 million, to any claim with a publicly traded party regardless of amount, and to qualified transactions and forum-clause contracts, with related arbitration proceedings under (d-1). Counter: the opponent prices the amount in controversy under $5 million after stripping interest, exemplary damages, penalties and fees, which the statute excludes from the count. Defeated by Property Code ch. 53 mechanic's-lien and Title 9 claims, which stay in district court at any size.
- **Small-lot entitlement suit** (SB 15 bullet). On an unplatted single-family tract of five acres or more in a city over 150,000 inside a county over 300,000, the developer plats 3,000-square-foot lots and, on refusal, sues under 211.058 for declaratory, mandamus and injunctive relief with mandatory fees. Counter: the city places the tract within 3,000 feet of an airport or military base (211.052(b)(2)) or points to HOA deed restrictions, which 211.057 leaves intact. Defeated by a platted tract, a tract under five acres, or a city or county under the population thresholds.
- **Healthcare noncompete re-papering** (SB 1318 bullet). Every physician, dentist, nurse and PA covenant entered or renewed on or after September 1, 2025 carries a buyout capped at annual salary and wages, a one-year term, a five-mile radius and written terms, or it is unenforceable. Counter: the departing physician pleads involuntary discharge without good cause, which voids the covenant under 15.50(d), or an administrative role outside (b-1). Defeated by a pre-September 1, 2025 covenant never renewed, which stays under the prior text.
- **Merchant-cash-advance compliance** (HB 700 bullet). A lender or fintech portfolio company offering sales-based financing under $1 million to Texas recipients registers with OCCC (existing businesses by December 31, 2026), delivers signed 398.051 disclosures, and strips confession-of-judgment clauses; exposure is a $10,000 civil penalty per violation with no private right of action, so the counterparty is OCCC under 7 TAC ch. 86, not the recipient. Counter: a recipient in default pleads that a confession-of-judgment clause is void under 398.055. Defeated by the bank, real-property-secured and lease exemptions in 398.003.
- **No agency deference** (SB 14 bullet). In a proceeding initiated on or after September 1, 2025, a portfolio company contesting a TDI, TSSB, OCCC, TCEQ or Comptroller reading of a statute gets de novo review of the legal question under 2001.042 and 2001.1721. Counter: the agency argues its reading is reasonable and consistent with plain text, which the court may still consider. Defeated by a proceeding initiated before September 1, 2025 (sec. 11).
- **BPP exemption per location** (Tax Code 11.145 bullet). Each Texas location's income-producing tangible personal property is exempt up to $125,000 of appraised value from January 1, 2026. Counter: the appraisal district aggregates related entities in a unified business enterprise under subsec. (f), so splitting inventory across affiliates at one location earns nothing. Defeated by value above the cap, taxed on the excess.
- **Homestead delta in housing models** (Tax Code 11.13 bullet). Owner-occupant school tax falls with the $140,000 exemption ($60,000 elderly or disabled add-on) from November 19, 2025, the canvass date on which SB 4 and SB 23 art. 1 took effect (each act's sec. 1.04 applies the change to tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025); investor-owned build-to-rent units get no homestead exemption, so the for-sale product's carrying cost moves against the rental thesis. Counter: none on the tax fact. Defeated by nothing in this file; pull the current 11.13 text at use.
- **Constitutional tax floor for exits and estates** (SJR 18, HJR 2, HJR 4 bullets and the operative-text bullets for §§ 24-b, 26, 30). Texas cannot tax an individual's, family's, estate's or trust's realized or unrealized capital gains (art. VIII § 24-b), impose death or gift taxes or expand one in effect on January 1, 2025 (§ 26), or tax securities transactions or registered market operators (§ 30) without a further amendment; founder exits, Texas Stock Exchange listings and estate plans run on that floor. Counter: entity-level gains sit outside § 24-b's text, and § 30(c) preserves general business taxes measured by business activity. Defeated by a later amendment, none of which can originate before the 90th Legislature convenes January 12, 2027.
- **Proxy-advisor leverage is enjoined as to Glass Lewis and ISS** (Glass Lewis v. Paxton bullet). The AG cannot enforce SB 2337 against Glass Lewis or intervene in private actions against it while the August 29, 2025 injunction stands, and ISS holds its own August 29, 2025 text-order injunction in the consolidated case, so a sponsor modeling AG-driven disclosure leverage against either advisor's recommendations has none. Counter: the orders are party-specific; a private plaintiff or an action against another advisor sits outside them. Defeated by dissolution at trial; trial settings were vacated September 2, 2026 pending reassignment.
- **TRAIGA and SB 16 as diligence items** (HB 149 and SB 16 bullets). From January 1, 2026 a portfolio company deploying AI systems to Texas consumers or employees is inside the ch. 551 civil-penalty regime; from December 4, 2025 real-property documents face new recording requirements and the offenses of real property theft and fraud. Both bullets verify caption and effective date only; the counter and the defeat live in operative text not read here (legal-ip and legal-cre carry the operative sections, see the Verified in column).
- **Planning horizon** (session calendar bullets). No Texas statute can be enacted before January 12, 2027 absent a called session; a portfolio compliance calendar through 2026 needs the effective dates in this file plus agency rulemaking, of which 7 TAC ch. 86 (effective July 9, 2026) is the one adoption seen.
