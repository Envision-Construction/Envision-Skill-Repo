## Sponsor angles

Each angle rests only on a VERIFIED bullet above and follows the house frame: mechanism, authority, the counterparty's best counter, what defeats the counter.

- **Form the operating entity in Texas when full fiduciary elimination matters.** Mechanism: the company agreement eliminates manager and officer duties outright. Authority: Tex. Bus. Orgs. Code § 101.401 (eff. May 14, 2025). Counter: a member argues the agreement's language falls short of elimination and default duties fill the gap. Defeats it: drafting that tracks the statutory verbs ("expand, restrict, or eliminate any duties, including fiduciary duties, and related liabilities") and names officers and other persons as the statute does.
- **Route Texas governance fights to the Business Court and keep lien fights out.** Mechanism: derivative, governance, and securities actions over $5 million go to a specialist bench; ch. 53 lien claims are excluded by (g)(2)(E). Authority: Tex. Gov't Code § 25A.004(b)-(d), (g)(2)(E). Counter: the counterparty pleads at or below $5 million or folds the dispute into a lien action. Defeats it: plead damages exclusive of interest, fees, and costs above the floor; plead lien claims in a separate action so the exclusion does not swallow the governance case.
- **Price owner-default risk into GC bids in North Carolina and Delaware; collect regardless as a sub.** Mechanism: pay-if-paid is unenforceable, and Delaware also voids non-Delaware law and forum clauses in subcontracts. Authority: N.C. Gen. Stat. § 22C-2; 6 Del. C. § 3507(e)(1), (e)(5). Counter: the GC invokes the residential carve-outs (§ 22C-6, 12 or fewer units; § 3507(f), 6 or fewer units). Defeats it: a commercial scope outside the carve-out, documented at contract.
- **Run the new Louisiana penalty clock as a contractor; as an owner, document the dispute.** Mechanism: since Aug. 1, 2026 the owner owes payment 35 days after a written request and 1.5% per month after, and a waiver is absolutely null. Authority: La. R.S. 9:2784(A)-(F) as amended by Acts 2026, No. 822. Counter: the owner asserts a good-faith dispute or a withholding "authorized by law, the contract, or this Section". Defeats it: split undisputed from disputed amounts in the request so the penalty runs on the undisputed balance; the only statutory extension is 61 days for single-family residences.
- **Take Georgia entity disputes to the State-wide Business Court after July 1, 2026.** Mechanism: internal entity claims joined the internal-affairs category for claims filed on or after that date, at $500,000 ($1,000,000 for commercial real property). Authority: O.C.G.A. § 15-5A-3(a)(1)(B) and Ga. L. 2026, p. 365, § 19/HB 1185. Counter: the counterparty contests the tier or leans on the editor's note that the 2026 act omitted existing (xi) language. Defeats it: plead the tier with the damages basis stated; treat the (xi) drafting gap as an ambiguity to brief, not a bar.
- **Calendar Form D state notices from the first in-state sale; New York is the outlier that files before selling.** Mechanism: GA, TX, CA, NC, DE, and TN each run 15 days from the first sale in that state with fees fixed or capped in the verified text (GA $250; TX 1/10 of 1% capped at $500; NC $350; DE $200 to $1,000; TN $500 with a rule-set late fee); NY treats the issuer as a dealer and bars sales until the state notice and broker-dealer statement are filed, with fees of $1,200 or $300. Authority: O.C.G.A. § 10-5-21(c); 7 TAC § 114.4(b)(1) with Gov't Code § 4006.052; Cal. Corp. Code § 25102.1(d); N.C. Gen. Stat. § 78A-31(b); 6 Del. C. § 73-208(b); Tenn. Defeats it: a per-state closing log fixing the first in-state sale date.
- **Pick the clawback horizon with the entity's state.** Mechanism: the applicable fraudulent-transfer act sets how far a creditor reaches back at a dividend recap. Authority: uniform four years plus a one-year discovery tail in GA, FL, TX, NC, NY, DE, NV, DC (Tennessee's insider period is four years); California adds a seven-year repose (Cal. Civ. - **Use the lien-deadline asymmetries as payment leverage.** Mechanism: the owner controls a recording that compresses the contractor's window in California (60 days after a notice of completion) and Nevada (40 days after a recorded notice of completion); the contractor controls its own protection in Tennessee (record within 90 days to beat later purchasers and lenders despite a one-year lien), Louisiana (a filed notice of contract earns the seven-month window), and Delaware (180 days with a direct owner contract, 120 for everyone else); North Carolina voids lien waivers given for a contract award. Authority: Cal. Civ. Code § 8412; NRS 108.226(1)(b); Tenn. Code § 66-11-112(a); La. R.S. 9:4822(C); 25 Del. C. § 2711; N.C. Gen. Stat. § 44A-12(f). Counter: the owner records the notice of completion early and argues the shorter window. Defeats it: calendar from the recording date, not the last day on site.
- **Choose the bench inside the state.** Mechanism: Nevada's Clark County business court takes entity disputes with no dollar test on a caption request; North Carolina's Business Court is mandatory at $5,000,000 and available by all-party consent at $1,000,000 for entity contract disputes; New York's Commercial Division thresholds range from $500,000 in New York County to $50,000 in Albany; Delaware's CCLD takes $1 Million claims and exclusive choice-of-court agreements; Tennessee's Davidson County docket takes $250,000 cases but out-of-county cases only on joint consent. Authority: EDCR 1.61; N.C. Gen. Stat. § 7A-45.4; 22 NYCRR 202.70(a); Del. Super. Ct. CCLD page; Business Court Guide § 2.1, § 3.1(b). Counter: the counterparty withholds the consent the NC and TN routes require. Defeats it: the consent written into the deal documents before the dispute exists.
